# Neurology — neurodegeneration and disease-modifying therapy.
# Real, well-established findings paraphrased from authoritative public sources
# (NINDS/NIH, NIA, PubMed/PMC). One self-contained statement per line.
Parkinson's disease is characterized neuropathologically by progressive loss of dopaminergic neurons in the substantia nigra pars compacta and by alpha-synuclein-containing Lewy bodies, with nigral neurons being selectively vulnerable relative to the neighboring ventral tegmental area.
Lecanemab, an anti-amyloid monoclonal antibody that binds soluble amyloid-beta protofibrils, slowed cognitive decline by about 27 percent over 18 months versus placebo in the Clarity AD trial of early Alzheimer's disease.
