# Precision oncology: driver mutations & targeted therapy
Activating EGFR mutations in the tyrosine kinase domain - most commonly exon 19 deletions and the exon 21 L858R point mutation - predict sensitivity to EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitors such as gefitinib in non-small cell lung cancer.
The EML4-ALK fusion, formed by an inversion on chromosome 2, occurs in roughly 3 to 5 percent of non-small cell lung cancers and produces a constitutively active kinase that responds to the ALK inhibitor crizotinib.
The KRAS G12C variant occurs in approximately 13 percent of lung adenocarcinomas; sotorasib covalently binds the mutant cysteine at position 12 and locks KRAS in its inactive GDP-bound state.
The Philadelphia chromosome, a t(9;22) translocation, creates the BCR-ABL1 fusion gene encoding a constitutively active tyrosine kinase that imatinib inhibits in chronic myeloid leukemia.
HER2 is overexpressed in about 15 to 20 percent of breast cancers, where gene amplification drives aggressive proliferation; the monoclonal antibody trastuzumab targets the HER2 receptor.
# DNA repair, immunotherapy biomarkers
Inherited harmful BRCA1 or BRCA2 variants impair homologous-recombination DNA repair and substantially raise breast and ovarian cancer risk; the resulting repair deficiency makes such tumors selectively vulnerable to PARP inhibitors through synthetic lethality.
Microsatellite instability-high (MSI-H) or mismatch-repair-deficient tumors carry a high mutational burden, and pembrolizumab received a tissue-agnostic approval in 2017 for these tumors regardless of their site of origin.
# Gene & molecular therapy
Casgevy (exagamglogene autotemcel), approved by the FDA in December 2023, uses CRISPR-Cas9 to disrupt the BCL11A enhancer in a patient's hematopoietic stem cells, reactivating fetal hemoglobin production in sickle cell disease.
Sickle cell disease arises from a single point mutation in the HBB gene that substitutes valine for glutamic acid at position 6 of beta-globin (Glu6Val), causing hemoglobin S to polymerize under low-oxygen conditions.
Ivacaftor is a CFTR potentiator that increases channel-opening probability in the class III G551D gating mutation, the most common gating mutation in cystic fibrosis, improving lung function in affected patients.
# Genetics of common disease
The APOE ε4 allele is the strongest known genetic risk factor for late-onset Alzheimer's disease, acting in a dose-dependent manner, while APOE ε2 is protective.
# Cardiometabolic & preventive medicine
Statins inhibit HMG-CoA reductase, the rate-limiting enzyme of hepatic cholesterol synthesis; meta-analyses show roughly a 21 to 22 percent reduction in major vascular events per 1 mmol/L reduction in LDL cholesterol.
Periconceptional folic acid supplementation of about 400 micrograms daily can prevent an estimated 50 to 70 percent of neural tube defects such as spina bifida and anencephaly.
# Microbiology
Helicobacter pylori, a spiral bacterium that colonizes the gastric lining, is the primary cause of peptic ulcer disease, a discovery by Marshall and Warren recognized with the 2005 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine.
