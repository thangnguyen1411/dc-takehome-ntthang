# Cardiometabolic & preventive medicine — mechanisms and outcome trials.
# Real, well-established findings paraphrased from authoritative public sources
# (NHLBI/NIH, NEJM, CDC, PubMed/PMC). One self-contained statement per line.
Statins inhibit HMG-CoA reductase, the rate-limiting enzyme of hepatic cholesterol synthesis; meta-analyses show roughly a 21 to 22 percent reduction in major vascular events per 1 mmol/L reduction in LDL cholesterol.
ACE inhibitors reduce mortality in heart failure with reduced ejection fraction by interrupting the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system, blocking the conversion of angiotensin I to the vasoconstrictor angiotensin II, as shown in the CONSENSUS and SOLVD trials.
Beta-blockers such as carvedilol and bisoprolol reduce sudden cardiac death and all-cause mortality in heart failure with reduced ejection fraction, as established by the MERIT-HF and COPERNICUS trials.
Metformin lowers blood glucose primarily by decreasing hepatic glucose production, an effect linked to activation of AMP-activated protein kinase (AMPK) in the liver.
In the SELECT trial, semaglutide, a GLP-1 receptor agonist, reduced major adverse cardiovascular events by about 20 percent in overweight or obese patients with established cardiovascular disease and without diabetes.
Periconceptional folic acid supplementation of about 400 micrograms daily can prevent an estimated 50 to 70 percent of neural tube defects such as spina bifida and anencephaly.
